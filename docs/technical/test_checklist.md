# Level 1 Test Checklist

1. Project opens without missing-file errors.
2. Intro story closes and player controls unlock.
3. Left/right movement, coyote jump and buffered jump work.
4. Quick attack destroys weak blocks after sufficient hits.
5. Held attack performs strong attack and damages reinforced support.
6. Ordinary melee contact is lethal without a seal.
7. Ranged projectile is lethal without a seal.
8. Artifact panel opens and both technique choices work.
9. Artifact grants one seal and 100 technique energy.
10. Dash damages weak objects and gives a short invulnerability window.
11. Hold Dash/Focus slows enemies and drains energy.
12. Technique projectile differs for Blade and Breaker.
13. Elite contact starts 3D duel.
14. High-ground, stealth, alarm and debris context affect the duel.
15. Guard, perfect parry, dodge, quick and heavy attacks function.
16. Elite win returns to the same 2D position.
17. Destroyed blocks, killed ordinary enemies and artifact stay changed after return.
18. Elite gate disappears after victory.
19. Checkpoint activates in upper court.
20. Boss duel starts and reaches phase two after the first clean hit.
21. Boss victory returns to 2D and opens the exit objective.
22. Exit completes the level and saves completion.
23. Restart button clears the current run.
