# Level 1 Story — Temple of the Rift

## Premise

Beneath an abandoned mountain temple, the Ash Mask has opened an unstable breach between the physical world and a sealed dueling plane. The hero follows the residual energy of a katana once carried by an unknown predecessor.

## Opening

The lower temple has partially collapsed. Ordinary raiders are extracting fragments from the breach and building makeshift fortifications. The hero enters through the service passage and learns movement, jumping, quick attacks, strong attacks and destruction.

## The Zero Seal Fragment

A hidden upper chamber contains the Zero Seal Fragment. It is not a living prisoner and does not grant a new character. The artifact reads the katana's resonance and lets the hero select one of two techniques:

- Blade — fast energy cut.
- Breaker — explosive talisman capable of destroying reinforced structures.

The artifact also restores one protective seal. The seal absorbs one otherwise lethal clean hit.

## Elite guardian

At the central gate, the Rift Guardian forces a Duel Shift. The way the player approaches changes the duel:

- attack from above weakens enemy posture;
- no alarm gives the player a first opening;
- destroying the reinforced support adds debris to the 3D arena;
- triggering an alarm shortens enemy telegraphs.

After victory, the player returns to the same 2D location. Destroyed blocks, defeated ordinary enemies and the collected artifact remain changed.

## Upper court

The opened gate leads to a vertical combat section with stronger ordinary enemies and ranged fire. The player reaches a checkpoint before the final duel.

## Boss

The boss is the Ash Mask Kensei. The first clean hit breaks the mask and starts phase two. In phase two the boss attacks faster and has more posture. A second clean hit ends the fight.

## Ending

The breach under the temple stabilizes, but the mask is revealed to be only a conduit. The remaining resonance points toward someone who knew the katana's previous owner. This creates the narrative hook for Level 2.
